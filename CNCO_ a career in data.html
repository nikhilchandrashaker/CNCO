Built from Wikipedia’s CNCO article and discography. The discography’s summary says 30 singles (8 as featured artist) and 6 promotional singles, but its tables list far more, so this page follows the tables. Where the article text and a table differ (Tan Fácil and Quisiera chart peaks, the date of the Que Quiénes Somos EP), the table or the main article is followed and the difference is noted. Some dates are only a month or a year. Chart peaks are Billboard Latin and Latin-market charts as listed in the source. Birth dates and music-video directors were not available in the sources used, so they are left out. Joel Pimentel’s year is counted through 2021 because he left in May. No streaming data is included.
